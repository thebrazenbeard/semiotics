## Separation rules

**Semantic Atlas:** Semiotics may emit candidate interpretations or sign-process records, but it does not create a second current-canon/adjudication mechanism.

**SPM:** Semiotics can provide representations and evaluation tasks. A registry is not a cognition substrate and does not own agent memory, authority, or action.

**UNVTRSLR:** Semiotics may contribute sign-process vocabulary and cases. A local sign registry does not solve grounding or universal translation.

**Discovery:** Adoption here does not promote a mechanism across the portfolio. Shared abstractions still have to earn promotion.
