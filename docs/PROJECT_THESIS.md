# Project Thesis

## Working question

What representation and evaluation discipline is needed to model a sign as a contextual relation among a sign vehicle, possible object or referent, interpretation, interpreter conditions, and evidence, without silently collapsing ambiguity or promoting interpretation into truth?

This repository treats semiotics as broader than word meaning. Candidate sign vehicles may be linguistic, visual, acoustic, embodied, behavioral, spatial, conventional, causal or indexical, iconic, mixed, or not yet classifiable.

## Design thesis

A useful computational semiotics layer should keep inspectable: the sign vehicle; the occurrence and context; possible objects or referents; the interpretant or reading; interpreter/community scope; evidence and provenance; unresolved alternatives; and the difference between interpretation, confidence, adjudication, authority, and action.

The v0.1 executable kernel implements only a subset: stable sign identities, explicit context tags, source-bound interpretations, live/rejected claim state, and non-authoritative support metadata.

## Non-goals

Semiotics is not a universal ontology, universal translator, replacement for Semantic Atlas, replacement for SPM, or authorization system. It does not claim that Peircean, Saussurean, structuralist, pragmatic, cognitive, or other semiotic theories are interchangeable or jointly proven.
