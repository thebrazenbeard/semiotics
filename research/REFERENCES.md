# Research References

Status: seed bibliography, not exhaustive literature review. Accessed 2026-09-28.

## External background

- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, **Peirce's Theory of Signs**: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/peirce-semiotics/
  - Background for sign/sign-vehicle, object, interpretant, and Peirce's evolving classifications.
- Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy, **Literary Theory**, Structuralism and Poststructuralism: https://iep.utm.edu/literary/
  - Secondary background on Saussurean signifier/signified, conventionality, and differential meaning.
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, **Medieval Semiotics**: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/semiotics-medieval/
  - Reminder that sign theory predates modern Peircean/Saussurean frameworks.

These sources motivate representation choices; they do not make the executable kernel a validated implementation of any historical theorist's complete system. Primary-text work and scholarly disagreement remain future research.
